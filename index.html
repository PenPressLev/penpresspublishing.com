The House

What we are for.

Pen & Press exists to steward, protect, and cultivate exceptional Christian creative work. Six standards govern the operation, and every piece that leaves the house answers to them.

We commit to building a legacy of wisdom, vision, and truth, shaping foundations for generations through integrity, innovation, and strategic stewardship.

Our vision is to see the Christian narrative restored to intellectual and cultural prominence, strengthening believers for the world they actually live in and multiplying faithful voices across the globe. Formation is the method. Scripture is the authority. Excellence is not optional.

  1. Faithful SovereigntyThe Integrity Standard

    Every work in our care is protected and valued, honoring both the creator and the Creator.

  2. Creative Counter-RevolutionThe Innovation Standard

    We foster radical originality and bold, imaginative expression, and use technology to carry the truth further.

  3. Systematic Equipping & DeploymentThe Empowerment Standard

    We provide tools and mentorship to develop gifts and mobilize creators into their full calling.

  4. Unflinching Transformational ContentThe Message Standard

    Our content is designed not only to inform, but to transform.

  5. Covenant Partnership & Generational FoundationThe Relational Standard

    We build lasting relationships with those who share the vision, for the sake of future generations.

  6. Absolute Operational ExcellenceThe Business Standard

    We operate with professionalism, clear communication, and ethical decision-making in every part of the business.

The Founder

Lev Montgomery

Born in the South Bronx in 1978 and trained at the High School of Graphic Communication Arts, a vocational printing school, Lev Montgomery learned ink and paper before he learned to publish either.

His writing spans devotional work, cultural commentary, fiction, and content architecture. He founded Pen & Press Holdings LLC and its publishing house, Pen & Press Publishing, to give that work a house with standards: a voice that is kept, a process that is governed, and a reader who is taken seriously.

He writes from Michigan, publishes The Pen & Press Vanguard on Substack, and keeps the hours listed above.

A pen is a small thing until someone decides what it is for.

Questions

Asked and answered.

The short answers, for readers and for anyone meeting the house for the first time.

What is Pen & Press Publishing?

Pen & Press Publishing is a Christian formation publishing house based in Michigan, founded in 2024 by Lev Montgomery. It publishes devotional books and The Pen & Press Vanguard on Substack. Pen & Press Publishing is a DBA of Pen & Press Holdings LLC.

Who is Lev Montgomery?

Lev Montgomery is a writer and the founder of Pen & Press Publishing. Born in the South Bronx in 1978 and trained at the High School of Graphic Communication Arts, he writes devotionals, cultural commentary, and fiction from Michigan.

What is Before the Day?

Before the Day is a 90-day devotional by Lev Montgomery for souls being quietly formed: ninety mornings, one passage at a time. It was released August 21, 2026, with a Companion Study Guide for fellowships and small groups.

What is A Light Has Come?

A Light Has Come: An Advent Vigil in the Glory of Christ Incarnate is a 21-day Advent devotional by Lev Montgomery, released November 12, 2026.

What is Morning Mastery?

Morning Mastery is the weekday devotional of Pen & Press Publishing, published on The Pen & Press Vanguard on Substack: one passage, one truth to carry, one prayer, written to be read before the day begins.

What are The Four Watches?

The Four Watches is the Scripture rail on this site. It shows a verse from the King James Version at midnight, sunrise, noon, and sunset, Eastern time, and moves through the whole Bible over thirty-one days.

Where can I buy the books?

The books are sold on Amazon and direct from the house. Churches, fellowships, and study groups can request a quote for bundles of Before the Day on this page.
